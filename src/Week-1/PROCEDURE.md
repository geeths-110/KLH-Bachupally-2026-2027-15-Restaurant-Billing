## Week-1 Procedure

## Title
Restaurant Order, Kitchen & Billing System

## Objective
To develop a simple Java program that accepts a customer's food order, calculates the total bill, and displays the kitchen order status.

## Problem
A restaurant needs a simple system to record customer orders, calculate the bill based on quantity and price, and show the order status from Pending to Ready.

## Input
Customer name
Food item
Quantity
Price per item

## Process
Accept the customer details and order details.
Store the food item, quantity, and price.
Calculate the total bill using quantity and price.
Display the order details.
Display the kitchen status.
Display the final bill.

## Output
Customer name
Food item
Quantity
Price per item
Kitchen order status
Total bill amount

## Algorithm
1.Start.
2.Create a Scanner object.
3.Read the customer name.
4.Read the food item.
5.Read the quantity.
6.Read the price per item.
7.Calculate total = quantity × price.
8.Display the order details.
9.Display the kitchen status as Pending, Preparing, and Ready.
10.Display the total bill.
11.Close the Scanner.
12.Stop.

## Concepts Used
Java program structure
Scanner for input
Variables
Data types
String input
Arithmetic operators
Multiplication
Assignment operator
System.out.println() for output
